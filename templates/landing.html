How It Works

1. Upload Evidence

Parties upload invoices, contracts, receipts, or chat logs. OCR and AI extract entities, amounts, dates, and timelines automatically.

2. Legal Research

The system retrieves relevant Indian statutes from verified sources including the Mediation Act 2023, Consumer Protection Act 2019, and more.

3. AI Mediation

AI identifies common ground and suggests fair settlement options without determining liability or blame.

4. Settlement Draft

A proposed Mediated Settlement Agreement is generated, including payment schedules and confidentiality clauses.

5. Admin Review

An administrator reviews the AI outputs, requests more evidence if needed, and approves or rejects the settlement draft.

6. PDF Export

Download a professionally formatted settlement agreement ready for signature and execution under Indian law.

Legal Framework

All legal references are grounded in authoritative Indian statutes:

  • Mediation Act, 2023
  • Consumer Protection Act, 2019
  • Indian Contract Act, 1872
  • Information Technology Act, 2000
  • MSMED Act, 2006

Sources: India Code (Official Government Repository)
